\section{Introduction}
Let $x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be a vector and let $f \colon \mathbb{R}^{n} \to \mathbb{R}$ be a smooth
function. We study the minimisers of the functional
\[
  J(u) = \int_{\Omega} \left( \tfrac{1}{2} \lvert \nabla u \rvert^{2} + V(u) \right) \, dx,
\]
under homogeneous boundary conditions. A classical reference is \cite{evans1998}.

\section{Results}
The main statement follows from a standard compactness argument; see \cite{brezis2011}
for the details. % TODO: check the constant in the estimate

\begin{equation}
  \lVert u \rVert_{H^{1}_{0}(\Omega)} \le C \, \lVert f \rVert_{L^{2}(\Omega)}.
  \label{eq:estimate}
\end{equation}

By \eqref{eq:estimate} the solution depends continuously on the data.
